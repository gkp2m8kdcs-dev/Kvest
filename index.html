A calm journal for your global portfolios.

Kvest is a private, on-device investment journal for active retail investors who want a single, focused workspace to track global equities, ETFs, and crypto across multiple strategy-driven portfolios.

Get in touch

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

No account, no cloud sync, no telemetry. Every trade, attachment, and note stays on your iPhone.

📈

Interactive Charts

Candlestick, line, and area styles with 1D to ALL time ranges, pinch-to-zoom, and a long-press crosshair revealing OHLC, volume, and exact timestamps.

💼

Multiple Portfolios

Build separate portfolios for long-term core holdings, growth bets, dividends, or any thesis you want isolated. Each gets its own allocation donut, holding list, and realized P&L.

🧾

Trade Logging

Log a buy or sell in seconds. Pick a portfolio, enter quantity and price, attach a photo of a receipt or chart, and Kvest immediately updates your cost basis and unrealized P&L.

🔒

Private by Design

All data is stored locally on your device. No logins, no notifications, no third-party dependencies. A one-tap reset lives in Settings whenever you want a fresh start.

Highlights

  • Cost basis, market value, and unrealized P&L recalculated instantly
  • Filter all trades by portfolio or direction
  • Average cost, current value, and per-name P&L on every position
  • Cash position and realized P&L tracked per portfolio
  • Custom dark theme with hand-drawn Core Graphics charts
  • Intuitive gestures: pinch, long-press, swipe
  • Receipts and chart snapshots attachable to any trade
  • One-tap data reset from Settings

Your data stays yours

Kvest is built around a simple principle: your financial journal is personal, and it should never leave your device unless you decide to export it.

📱

On-device storage

Trades, notes, and attachments are stored locally on your iPhone. We don't operate servers that hold your data.

🚫

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no third-party telemetry. Kvest doesn't know who you are.

🗝️

You stay in control

Reset everything with a single tap from Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data permanently.
