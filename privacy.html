Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Short version: Kvest does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. All your trades, notes, and attachments are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

1. Who we are

Kvest ("we", "us", "our") is a private, on-device investment journal for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

2. Information we collect

Kvest is designed to function without a server account and without transmitting your data off your device. Specifically:

  • Account data: We do not require an account, email, password, or any sign-in.
  • Portfolio & trade data: Trades, holdings, notes, portfolios, and any associated attachments you create in the app are stored locally on your iPhone using standard iOS storage.
  • Device permissions: Kvest may request access to the camera or photo library solely so you can attach a receipt, chart snapshot, or note image to a trade. We do not upload these images to any server.
  • Usage analytics: Kvest does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or telemetry. We do not track how you use the app.
  • Diagnostics: We do not collect crash reports or diagnostic data.

3. How we use your information

We use information only as needed to operate the app on your device:

  • To display your portfolios, holdings, and trades within the app.
  • To compute cost basis, market value, and realized / unrealized P&L from your own entries.
  • To attach images you choose from your photo library or capture with the camera.

Market prices and chart data shown inside the app are generated locally from on-device routines and do not depend on personal information.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any personal information with third parties. Since Kvest does not collect personal information on a server, there is nothing to share. Images and notes you create remain on your device unless you explicitly export them yourself.

5. Children

Kvest is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Reset: A one-tap reset in Settings permanently clears all portfolios, trades, and attachments stored by Kvest on your device.
  • Uninstall: Removing Kvest from your iPhone deletes all data associated with the app.
  • Permissions: You can revoke camera or photo library access at any time in iOS Settings. Kvest will continue to work without those permissions, but you won't be able to attach new images.
  • Export: Any data export is initiated by you. Kvest does not perform automatic backups to cloud services.

7. Data security

Data you store in Kvest is protected by iOS sandboxing and the device's standard security features, including the passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID you have set on your iPhone. Because data does not leave your device by default, the risk surface is limited to the device itself.

8. International use

Kvest is distributed globally through the App Store. Because we do not collect personal data, your region does not affect what we hold, because we hold nothing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Because Kvest does not collect data on a server, we cannot notify you directly within the app; please check this page periodically.
